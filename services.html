What we can help you with

These are areas we work in—we'll confirm fit, timeline, and risk on a call before anything is promised on paper.

Workflow and process automation

If your team lives in report exports, reconciliations, approvals, or statement handling, we can explore automation—scheduled jobs, checks, and handoffs you own.

  • Scheduled jobs and exception alerts
  • Human-in-the-loop steps where approvals matter
  • Documentation so your team owns the runbook

Data pipelines and integrations

If you need brokers, CRMs, payment gateways, or spreadsheets to stay in sync, we can scope APIs, scripts, validation, and monitoring—at a size that matches your stage.

  • Ingestion, validation, and monitoring
  • Idempotent jobs and error recovery
  • Security-minded handling of credentials and PII

Custom internal tools and dashboards

Admin panels, analytics views, and small internal apps—we'll propose something maintainable rather than a one-off nobody can extend later.

  • Role-based access and audit-friendly views
  • Operational KPIs your leadership actually uses
  • Iterative delivery from pilot to wider rollout

Web data collection (where appropriate)

For lawful, scoped needs, we can pair custom work with OneCrawl or a lighter script—never a blanket "scrape anything" offer.

  • Scope, robots.txt, and terms of use reviewed with you
  • Rate limits and stable export formats
  • Often paired with our OneCrawl product direction
