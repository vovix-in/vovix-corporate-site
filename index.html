Sample projects

Illustrative examples of the kind of client work we take on—details anonymized; results depend on scope and your environment.

Broker ops (example)

Daily P&L and client statements

Problem: A small broker spent hours each day exporting positions, formatting statements, and checking totals by hand.

What we built: Automated pulls from their stack, reconciliation checks, and PDF statements generated on a schedule with exception queues.

Result: Roughly 2–3 hours of manual work per day removed from the ops desk, with clearer audit trails.

SaaS internal admin (example)

Single place for users, refunds, and plans

Problem: A growing SaaS team juggled spreadsheets and three consoles to change plans, process refunds, and suspend accounts.

What we built: A lightweight internal admin with role-based access, unified customer view, and guarded actions against their billing API.

Result: Support and founders handled lifecycle changes faster with fewer mistakes and less context switching.

Pharmacy retail (example)

Margin alerts from POS vs purchase data

Problem: A regional chain compared selling prices to landed cost in spreadsheets every week; fast movers and expiry risk were easy to miss.

What we built: Nightly ingest from POS and supplier invoices, margin rules by category, and a short exception list for the purchase manager.

Result: Weekly review time dropped sharply and buying decisions used one trusted sheet instead of three exports.
