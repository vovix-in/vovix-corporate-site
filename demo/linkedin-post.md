## Long / founder voice (≈260 words)

Three years of watching analysts copy-paste numbers from PDFs into Excel.

Three years of watching the same workflow break the same way: an annual
report drops at 6pm, the analyst pulls 30 numbers into a workbook by
midnight, and the next morning someone asks "where did this Net Profit
figure come from?" — and the answer is a screenshot.

That's the problem we built Vovix OneCrawl to solve.

Paste a filings URL — NSE, BSE, SEC EDGAR, or a direct PDF link. OneCrawl
crawls the source, downloads the filings, parses layout, runs OCR on the
financial tables, and streams structured rows into your workbook.
Revenue, EBITDA, Net Profit, EPS, D/E, ROE — plus YoY%, 3Y CAGR, source
page, and a confidence score on every cell.

The differentiator isn't speed. Most tools are fast. The differentiator
is **auditability**: every number is linked back to the exact page in
the original filing. Open a row, see the source. That's the part research
and compliance teams told us they actually needed.
