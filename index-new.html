Monday reconciliation, across three exports.

A · Situation

An operations lead consolidates three CSV extracts each Monday to review cash and positions. Manual stitching introduces error risk as volume grows.

B · First slice

Automate consolidation, surface exceptions, deliver one summary view — before any larger build is committed.
