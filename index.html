The problem

Where the hours actually go

Most ops teams don't have a software problem. They have a dozen small manual habits that quietly consume a working week every month.

Today — by hand

Re-keying invoices and statements into Tally or the ERP
Reconciling three exports before a Monday review
Copy-pasting between a CRM, a portal, and a spreadsheet
Assembling the same report every week from scratch
Chasing approvals over email with no audit trail

After — automated

Documents read once, posted straight into your system
Scheduled jobs reconcile and flag only the exceptions
Systems stay in sync over APIs, with retries and monitoring
Reports build themselves and land where you already work
Approvals routed to a human, recorded, and traceable

What we do

What we automate

Four areas where a small, well-built system reliably beats a manual process. If your problem isn't one of these, we'll say so.

Document & data entry automation

Invoices, receipts, bank statements, KYC records and purchase orders read once — every line item, table and tax breakup — then posted into TallyPrime, your ERP, or Excel. No templates, no manual keying.

  • Any format and any language — photo, scan or PDF
  • GSTIN validation and tax-field checks built in
  • Low-confidence fields flagged for a human to confirm

Runs on our own engine, Vovix Lens.

Data pipelines & integrations

When your CRM, payment gateway, vendor portal and spreadsheets each hold part of the truth, we build the connective tissue that keeps them agreeing with each other.

  • Ingestion, validation and monitoring as one system
  • Idempotent jobs, retries and sane error recovery
  • Security-minded handling of credentials and PII

Delivered as JSON, webhooks or a documented REST API.

Internal tools & dashboards

Admin panels, operational KPIs and small internal apps — built to be maintainable and extended by your team later, not a one-off nobody can touch.

  • Role-based access and audit-friendly views
  • The operational numbers your leadership actually opens
  • Iterative delivery from pilot to wider rollout

Handed over with schema docs and a runbook.

Scheduled jobs & web data collection

Recurring extracts, monitoring and alerting — plus lawful, scoped data collection where a portal has no API. We never offer a blanket "scrape anything" engagement.

  • Scope, robots.txt and terms of use reviewed with you
  • Rate limits, stable export formats, anti-brittleness
  • Exception alerts instead of silent failures

Scope agreed in writing before we build.

Every engagement starts with a written scope — including the parts we'd advise you not to automate yet.

How it works

From manual process to running system

Three clear steps. We map the real process first, build with the checks already in place, and leave you owning it.

01

Map the manual work

We walk your actual process, count where the hours go, and agree what should be automated — and what shouldn't. Sources, volumes, compliance constraints and success metrics, all in writing before we build.

02

Build with checks in place

Jobs, validation rules, retries, monitoring and alerts engineered in from day one — plus human approval steps wherever a wrong automated decision would be expensive.

03

Hand it over

It runs in your stack and delivers in your format — JSON, webhooks, REST API, or straight into Tally and your ERP. Full schema documentation and an integration runbook your team owns.
