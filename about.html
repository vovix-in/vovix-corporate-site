Who we are

Vovix is a Chennai-based company that builds software tools to automate tasks and support operational decisions for Indian businesses—the same framing we use on our legal pages, because it is accurate.

Who we want to work with: brokers, SaaS teams, and SMEs that need stable automation—reports, reconciliations, integrations, and internal dashboards—without vaporware.

Our services page explains what we are open to building for you. Products OneView and OneCrawl are what you can try today; Vovix Labs is where we experiment with market-facing ideas.
