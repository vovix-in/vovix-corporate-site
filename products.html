AI signals for MetaTrader 5

Beta · Early access

Vovix Edge

Trade forex with discipline — not guesswork. Vovix Edge is an AI-assisted signal app for MetaTrader 5 that surfaces only high-quality setups, sized to your account — and tells you when to stand down.

Most signal tools flood you with alerts. Vovix Edge does the opposite. It reads live news, price structure, and market conditions in real time — and only shows you a trade when the trend, the risk-to-reward, and the timing genuinely line up. Every signal isn't a vague tip: it's a clear plan — entry, stop, target, and position size, priced to your broker and your balance.

Only A+ setups. A strict quality gate filters for trades with the trend, in the right regime, with real reward-to-risk — and skips the rest
AI + technicals, together. News sentiment and technical structure combined into one clear read
Risk-first by design. Every signal comes sized to your account, with a defined stop and target — risk control is built in
One-click assisted execution. Place on MT5 and let the app manage the exit — partial profit, breakeven, trailing (opt-in, with a kill switch and daily limits)
News-aware. Built-in economic calendar and event awareness help you avoid trading into surprises
Private by design. Desktop-only for Windows + MT5 — your broker credentials stay on your machine

The difference: it knows when not to trade. Anyone can generate signals. In unclear, low-quality, or high-risk conditions, Vovix Edge deliberately shows nothing. Fewer, better opportunities — discipline over noise.

Document intelligence for CA firms

Live

Vovix Lens

A full document-extraction engine for chartered accountant firms and finance teams. Upload an invoice, receipt, bank statement or KYC record — a photo, a scan, or a PDF, in any language — and Vovix Lens returns every field, table and total as clean, verified data, posted straight into Tally, your ERP, or Excel. No templates. No manual keying.

Any document, any language: invoices, receipts, bank statements, PAN/Aadhaar & KYC, salary slips and purchase orders
Complete extraction: every line item, table and tax breakup captured verbatim — not just headline fields
One-click Tally & ERP export: post vouchers into TallyPrime, or export ready-to-import XML, Excel and Sheets
GST-ready: GSTIN validation and tax-field intelligence built in
Source-grounded accuracy: every value maps to its exact location on the original document
Human-in-the-loop review: low-confidence fields are flagged for a quick check — you stay in control
