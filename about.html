Founder's note

Why Vovix

I started Vovix in 2026 because too many ops teams still lose time to exports, reconciliations, and one-off integrations that should be small, reliable systems — not weekly heroics in spreadsheets.

We're deliberately early-stage: three products you can try today, and only a handful of custom engagements at a time so we can scope in writing and ship without pretending we already have enterprise-scale playbooks.

If that sounds like the kind of honesty you want from a vendor, get in touch. We'd rather turn down a bad fit than oversell one.

The team

Who you'll work with

Vovix is founder-led: discovery, engineering, and delivery go through the same small team. There are no separate "Leadership," "Engineering," and "Customer Success" departments on paper. When we outgrow that model, we'll say so here.
